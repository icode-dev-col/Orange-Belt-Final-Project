Tales of the Gold Dust

The Celestial Descent

According to the scrolls of the First Elves, pixie are not entirely native to the mortal plane. During the celestial wars, fragments of shattered moonlight fell into the mystical springs of the deep woodlands, coalescing over centuries into these magnificent Pixies.

Healing and Grace

The horn of a Pixie contains immense restorative properties. A single touch can cure any poison andmend the most grievous of wounds. However, to take Gold dust by force brings a curse of eternal misfortune upon the thief and their lineage.

True healers know that simply being in the presence of a Sprit Pixie is often enough to purify the darkest afflictions of both body and spirit.
