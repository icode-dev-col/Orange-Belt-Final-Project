Tales of the Silver Horn

The Celestial Descent

According to the scrolls of the First Elves, unicorns are not entirely native to the mortal plane. During the celestial wars, fragments of shattered moonlight fell into the mystical springs of the deep woodlands, coalescing over centuries into these magnificent beasts.

Healing and Grace

The horn of a unicorn contains immense restorative properties. A single touch can cure any poison andmend the most grievous of wounds. However, to take a horn by force brings a curse of eternal misfortune upon the thief and their lineage.

True healers know that simply being in the presence of a willing unicorn is often enough to purify the darkest afflictions of both body and spirit.
