The Dragon

Lord of the Crimson Peaks

Visage of Fire

Behold the majestic form of the ancient dragon. Its scales shimmer with the heat of a thousand molten mountains. Once a guardian of the first kingdoms, it now slumbers amongst the ruins, waiting for an age of magic to return.

Its eyes glow with the accumulated wisdom of millennia, piercing through deceit and fear alike. Very few have gazed upon the dragon and lived to tell the tale, and those who do are forever changed by its overwhelming presence.
